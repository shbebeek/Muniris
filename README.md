# Muniris
A couch co-op multiplayer shooter built using Unity. Has a mix of RPG puzzle-solving and action mechanics, combined with semi-precise platforming.

### Releases:
1. Download for Windows here: https://gamejolt.com/games/muniris-windows/1100476
   - Click download and run the Muniris.exe in order to play the game.
   - Seriously recommended to close all other applications.
3. Download for Mac here: https://gamejolt.com/games/muniris/1100442
   - Click download and run the file in order to play the game.
   - Also recommended to close all other applications.

### Multiplayer Setup:
- Connect a controller to play on the left-hand side of the screen, and play with a keyboard and mouse to play on the right side.  
- If you just want to play single-player use a keyboard to control the right camera.

### Features / How To Play
1. Players have 2 hands, a left and right hand.
   - You don't start off with a left hand item, but you can use the left and right hands in conjunction.
   - Left hand is for magic items, right hand is for gun weapons.
2. Players share a pooled magic system.
   - When one player uses a magic item, they deplete both player's remaining magic.
   - Hit enemies with bullets to regain magic.
3. Explore the world to find health, stamina, and magic upgrades, as well as "tap" chests that contribute towards your completion percentage.
4. Assign left and right hand items using the inventory menu.
5. Center your gun in order to get better aim at the cost of not being able to use your left hand.
6. Crawl around on the floor in order to hide from enemies and fit through small spaces.

### Tips and Tricks
1. The Mac release has better framerate optimization than the browser version.
2. Complete all of the forest areas first before moving on to other zones, it helps with the platforming and enemy challenge.
3. Don't forget you have left-hand items to use!

### Controls
#### Keyboard
- Jump [Space]
- Movement [WASD]
- Camera [Mouse]
- Shoot [Left Mouse]
- Reload [R]
- Pick Up / Interact [E]
- Use Left Hand [Q]
- Center gun [TAB]
- Run [Left Shift]
- Crouch [Left Command]
- Crawl [Left Control]
- Inventory [Enter]
- Dash [Right Mouse] (need to get upgrade in order to use)
#### Controller
- Jump [Button South]
- Movement [Left Toggle]
- Camera [Right Toggle]
- Shoot [Right Trigger]
- Reload [Button West]
- Pick Up / Interact [Button East]
- Use Left Hand [Left Trigger]
- Center gun [Click Right Toggle]
- Run [Click Left Toggle]
- Crouch [Left Bumper]
- Crawl [D-Pad Down]
- Inventory [+]
- Dash [Button North] (need to get upgrade in order to use)
